## 2.4 Interfaces

The interface with Chapter 3 is the set of simulated scenarios. Every Chapter 3 rung walk covers each simulated scenario that §§2.1–2.3 enumerate under its rung, in the nested-list form set out in §3.0.3.

The interface with Chapter 1 is the Threat context. The report defines a Threat context as a geopolitical or natural condition (peacetime accident, blockade/being surrounded, PLA landing, mega-quake) discussed in the Background chapter only, and treats it as causally decoupled from the disruption scenarios: any Threat context might produce any disruption scenario, or none. Geopolitical and natural-hazard conditions therefore frame the report for the reader in Chapter 1 and are not used in Chapter 2 or Chapter 3 as analytical axes or causal inputs; a section that names a Threat context as the cause of a disruption rung crosses this boundary.
